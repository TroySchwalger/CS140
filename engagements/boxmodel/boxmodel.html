What does the box say?

  • The differences between a block tag and an inline tag is that a block tag starts a new line such as the paragraph tag, horizontal line tag, and the list tags. Div tags.
  • The Inline tag are tags that dont break up the lines and can be placed in the middle of a text such as anchor tags and image tags. Others are italics or em tags and bold or strong tags Span tags.
  • The margin is outside of the box
  • The border is the border of the box
  • The padding is the inside of the box
  • Different units of measurement are px, cm, in, mm, and by percentage
