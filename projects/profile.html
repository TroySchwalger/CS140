Монголын Номлол

I was a missionary in the Mongolia Ulaanbaatar mission from 2023-2025. During this time I served in 4 different city areas and 3 different huduu areas. Of these different area, my favorites would be Baganuur, Sainshand in the East Gobi Province, and Dalanzadgad of the South Gobi Province.

I had the opportunity to be companions with 14 other missionaries. 10 of them were from America and 4 of them were from Mongolia. Unlike most English names, Mongolian names are made out of words. For example, Saikhanbeleg, where Saikhan means beautiful, and beleg means gift.
