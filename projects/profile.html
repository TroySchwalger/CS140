Монголын Номлол

I was a missionary in the Mongolia Ulaanbaatar mission from 2023-2025. During this time I served in 4 different city areas and 3 different huduu areas. Of these different area, my favorites would be Baganuur, Sainshand in the East Gobi Province, and Dalanzadgad of the South Gobi Province. The youth in Baganuur are very fun and tightknit and evergrowing. The geography of both of my gobi desert areas were fascinating, with Sainshand kicking up sandstorms and Dalanzadgad being known for dinosaurs.

I had the opportunity to be companions with 14 other missionaries. 10 of them were from America and 4 of them were from Mongolia. Unlike most English names, Mongolian names are made out of words. For example, Saikhanbeleg, where Saikhan means beautiful, and beleg means gift.
