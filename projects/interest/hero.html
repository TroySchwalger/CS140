SmallAnt

Interests Heroes

One of the most well known Super Mario Odyssey players is a youtuber named SmallAnt. At the start of his youtube career in Super Mario Odysey, he was big into trick jumping and speedrunning the game. His fastest Any% time is 1:03:25. Nowadays, whenever he plays Mario Odyssey, he plays a mod called a randomizer. He usually does races with friends or does other kinds of games such as Task Masters or other kinds.

Any% speedrun
The quickest way to beat a game. It does not require getting eveything.
Randomizer
All of the moves and objectives have been switched around for this mode.
Taskmaster
A british gameshow where a guy gives contestants random tasks and then judges them on how well they did it.
