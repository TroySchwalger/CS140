Interest on Super Mario Odyssey

Interests Heroes

I enjoy playing a lot of video games, especially Nintendo game. One of Nintendos most popular franchises is the Mario franchise about a plumber trying to save a princess from an evil turtle. Most of the Mario games are 2D platformers, but there some 3D platformers in the franchise.

Each 3D Mario game introduces a new gimmick that the game is focused on. In Mario Odyssey, the gimmick is a new companion for Mario named Cappy. As the name suggests, Cappy is a cap. Mario can throw Cappy around and bounce on him, but the most prominent feature is the ability to capture creatures.

There are many creatures in Mario Odyssey such as the goomba, bullet bill, and uproot. Each of these unique creatures, when captured, help Mario traverse the different kingdoms and collect power moons in order to save the princess.
