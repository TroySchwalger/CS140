Super Smash Bros is a video game franchise that is composed of characters from multiple series ranging from Mario to Minecraft. The original cast of Super Smash Bros featured 12 playable characters. The latest Super Smash Bros game features all previous playable characters and more making it stand at a whopping 82 playable characters!

There are currently 5 games in the franchise. The first game, Super Smash Bros., is on the nintendo 64. The next one, Super Smash Bros Melee, is on the gamecube. Super Smash Bros Brawl is on the Wii. Super Smash Bros for the Wii U and 3DS which is also known as SSB4, is played on the Wii U and 3DS. The last one, Super Smash Bros Ultimate, is played on the nintendo switch.
